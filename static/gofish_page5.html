How to Play

To play

  1. Press start button to begin.
  2. Press the hook button to the direction you want.
  3. The hook will pull up the fish itself if your hook touch the fish.
  4. You have the timer which limit your time to catch the fish on the top right game screen.
  5. You have to reach the target points given to you during the time limit.
  6. If you reach the target points before the time runs out, you win!
  7. If not game over and have to restart the game!

Fish & Points

Guppy(Small)
10 points
Tilapia(Medium)
20 points
Betta(Big)
30 points

To upgrade

Upgrade your fishing gear in Tools section to catch bigger fish and earn more points!

Game is saved automatically by your name and age for next game. You can resume the game at any time.
